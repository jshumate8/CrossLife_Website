Statement of faith

What we believe

We hold to the historic Christian faith as summarized in the ancient creeds, and we teach it from the text of Scripture.

Apostles’ CreedNicene CreedChalcedonian Definition

The Scriptures

We believe the Bible, Old and New Testaments, is the inspired, inerrant Word of God. It is fully sufficient for faith and life, and it is the final authority over every teaching and tradition.

2 Timothy 3:16–17 · 2 Peter 1:20–21 · Psalm 19:7–11

God

We believe in one God, eternally existing in three persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, equal in power and glory. He is the sovereign Creator and Lord of all, working all things according to the counsel of His will.

Deuteronomy 6:4 · Matthew 28:19 · Ephesians 1:11

Humanity and Sin

We believe God created man and woman in His image. Through Adam’s fall, sin entered the world, and all people are by nature sinful, separated from God, and unable to save themselves.

Genesis 1:27 · Romans 3:23 · Romans 5:12 · Ephesians 2:1–3

Jesus Christ

We believe Jesus Christ is the eternal Son of God, fully God and fully man, born of a virgin. He lived a sinless life, died on the cross as a substitute for sinners, rose bodily from the dead, and ascended to the right hand of the Father, where He reigns and intercedes for His people.

John 1:1, 14 · 1 Corinthians 15:3–4 · Hebrews 7:25

Salvation by Grace

We believe salvation is entirely the work of God’s grace. Before the foundation of the world, the Father chose a people in Christ. The Son laid down His life for them. The Spirit gives new life to dead hearts and draws them to faith. Everyone who repents and believes is saved, and God will keep to the end all those He has saved.

Ephesians 1:4–5 · John 6:37, 44 · John 10:11, 27–29 · Titus 3:5 · Philippians 1:6

Justification by Faith

We believe sinners are declared righteous before God by grace alone, through faith alone, in Christ alone. Christ’s righteousness is credited to us, not because of any work of our own.

Romans 3:21–26 · Romans 5:1 · 2 Corinthians 5:21 · Ephesians 2:8–9

The Holy Spirit

We believe the Holy Spirit regenerates, indwells, and sanctifies every believer, conforming us to the image of Christ. We believe the Spirit continues to give all of His gifts to the church today for its building up. They are to be used in love, in good order, and always in submission to Scripture, which no gift or experience adds to or overrules.

John 16:13–14 · Romans 8:9–11 · 1 Corinthians 12:4–11 · 1 Corinthians 14:1, 40

The Church

We believe the church is the body of Christ, made up of all true believers. Christians are called to belong to a local church for worship, the preaching of the Word, fellowship, mutual care, and mission. Christ gave His church two ordinances: baptism and the Lord’s Supper.

Matthew 28:18–20 · Acts 2:42 · 1 Corinthians 11:23–26 · Hebrews 10:24–25

Last Things

We affirm Christ’s personal, visible, bodily return to judge the living and the dead and to make all things new. Those in Christ will be raised to eternal life with Him, and those apart from Him to eternal judgment.

Acts 1:11 · John 5:28–29 · 1 Thessalonians 4:16–17 · Revelation 21:1–5

Where we hold liberty

CrossLife serves believers from many church traditions. On the following questions, faithful Christians who share the convictions above have reached different conclusions, and we teach with charity toward each view:

  • Baptism: its proper subjects and mode.
  • Church government: how local churches are led and organized.
  • The millennium: we hold amillennial and historic premillennial views as faithful readings of Scripture.
