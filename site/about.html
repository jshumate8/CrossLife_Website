About CrossLife

Our heart for biblical teaching and discipleship

Our mission

CrossLife exists to equip believers with sound biblical teaching and practical study materials. We believe in the transforming power of God’s Word, and we are committed to making it accessible and understandable for everyone who wants to grow in their faith, whatever language they speak.

Our vision

We envision believers who are rooted in Scripture, growing in likeness to Christ, and equipped to share the gospel with others. Through Bible studies, theological teaching, and discipleship resources, we aim to help Christians understand God’s Word and apply it to everyday life.

How we teach

Our approach

Text first

We start with what the Bible says, in context, and let doctrine come from the text, not the other way around.

Clear on the gospel

We teach the historic Christian faith plainly: salvation by grace alone, through faith alone, in Christ alone.

Charitable on secondary matters

We serve believers from many church traditions and hold some questions with liberty. See where.
